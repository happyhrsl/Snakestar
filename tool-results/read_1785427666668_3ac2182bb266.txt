    23→    23→ *  - Wall = death. The map has a breathing circular boundary
    24→    24→ *    (radius 3800 ± 40, cycle 10 s). Rendered in render-helpers as a neon
    25→    25→ *    rose circle. Hitting it kills you.
    26→    26→ *  - World is 8000×8000, centered at (4000, 4000).
    27→    27→ *
    28→    28→ * High-level architecture:
    29→    29→ *  - One socket effect (mount-once per arenaId) wires every server event with
    30→    30→ *    a paired `.off(...)` cleanup. On `connect`/`reconnect` we re-emit
    31→    31→ *    `join_arena` (the old canvas never did, leaving the player a ghost after
    32→    32→ *    a blip). [FIXES C7, C8, S1, S2, S3]

    34→    34→ *    DPR-aware sizing. The loop reads purely from refs (no closures over
    35→    35→ *    stale state). rAF is cancelled on unmount.
    36→    36→ *  - One input effect (mount-once) wires mouse / keyboard / touch joystick
    37→    37→ *    with `preventDefault` for arrows/space, blur-reset for stuck keys,
    38→    38→ *    pointer-capture on the extract & boost buttons, and a virtual joystick
    39→    39→ *    on the bottom-left quadrant of the canvas.
    40→    40→ *  - Rendering math lives in `./render-helpers.ts` (pure functions, no React).
